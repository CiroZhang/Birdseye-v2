Teacher and Student

Teacher and student, in 3D

Student (gold shuttle, solid bodies, orange rackets): one network that sees only the TV feed, trained to reproduce the teacher. Teacher (everything in blue): fitted from all four cameras: TV, the fixed side camera and both player cameras. It labels only what it is sure of: bright blue frames are labels, grey-blue ones are left out. These rallies were kept out of the student's training, so it has never seen their labels. Play or scrub the video, or click the timeline, and the 3D scene follows. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
